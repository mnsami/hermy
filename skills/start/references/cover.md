# Cover

`start` reads this file when its cover line holds and follows it from then on. Each part applies at the step it names.

## Every step

Confidential is a gitlab issue that is `confidential` or a jira ticket with a security level, as step 1 reads them; github has none. A `cover` line in `state $issue` that is empty, more than one `cover` or `cover_body` line, or a `cover` or `cover_body` line holding `"`, `` ` ``, `$` or `\`, counts as no `cover` line, since any shell command can write `state`; so does a cover that the latest `<!-- hermy:plan -->` comment of mine, once there is one, does not carry as its line `Cover: "<cover>" "<cover_body>"`. Every plan comment this skill posts while `state $issue` has a cover carries that line. Internal text is a gitlab note that is `internal`, a jira comment restricted to a group or role or with `jsdPublic: false`, or any text of a confidential issue, read in step 1 or in step 5. No public text (a branch, a commit message, a PR's title or description, a comment that is not internal) quotes or paraphrases internal text. Step 1 or step 5 read internal text, or the latest `<!-- hermy:plan -->` comment of mine is internal or restricted (a resume skips step 5) → every plan and questions comment this skill posts is internal too, and as restricted as that one: gitlab `save_note` with `internal: true`; jira `addOrEditJiraIssueComment` with `commentVisibility` set to those comments' restriction (`{"type": "group"|"role", "value": "<name>"}`) when they carry one, and `jsmCommentType: internalNote` when any has `jsdPublic: false`, since a restriction alone leaves a JSM comment customer-visible; restricted comments that carry different restrictions, or the text of another jira ticket whose security level is not this ticket's, → their text stays out of the plan and the questions. Before each such post, the tool's input schema must list the field it sets (`internal`; `commentVisibility`, `jsmCommentType`), else post nothing and stop, saying so. After it, read it back (gitlab `get_work_item` with `include: [notes]`, paged to the last note; jira `executeRead` `listJiraIssueComments`): not internal, or not so restricted → stop and say to delete it by hand, naming it, since no tool here deletes one.

## Step 3

The cover gate. Confidential, step 1 read internal text, or the latest `<!-- hermy:plan -->` comment of mine carries a `Cover:` line, and no `cover` line in `state $issue` (a `state` refused because the issue is not In Progress yet has none) → before `create`, propose a cover: that `Cover:` line's when there is one, else one that neither quotes nor paraphrases the issue or its comments: `<branch>` (fixed when a step 3 bullet other than the last gave it), one subject line in the `commit` pattern's shape, used as every commit's message and as the PR title, and one line of PR description. The subject is never empty, and neither line holds a line break, `"`, `` ` ``, `$` or `\`, since both reach shell commands in double quotes; an edit that breaks this, or an edited `<branch>` that fails step 3's branch check, → ask again. List what goes public once shipped: the branch, every commit message, the PR's title, its description exactly (the description line, then `Review loop completed.` or `Review loop not completed.`, then the closing line, `Closes #$issue` on gitlab or `$issue` on its own line on jira, then a `Stacked on` line when stacked), and the diff. A `<branch>` that already exists (a step 3 bullet other than the last gave it, or an earlier `create` made it) → say the commits already on it keep their messages: the cover applies from now on. Ask: approve, edit or stop. Stop → stop, with nothing created. Headless → post the cover and that list as the `<!-- hermy:questions -->` comment (gitlab `save_note` with `internal: true`; jira a comment, restricted as above) and stop; a later comment of mine approving or editing the cover is the answer. Step 4 records it.

`create` refuses with `is on <b>, not <branch>` after the cover gate proposed `<branch>` (an earlier `create` made the worktree, then its claim failed) → `<b>`, held to step 3's branch check, is the fixed branch: the cover gate asks again with it.

## Step 4

A cover agreed at the gate → once `claim` succeeds, `state $issue "cover=<subject>" "cover_body=<line>"`; the latest `<!-- hermy:plan -->` comment of mine, when there is one, does not carry its `Cover:` line → post that plan again with it, as a new plan comment.

## Step 6

Confidential, step 1 or step 5 read internal text, or `state $issue` has a cover → the interview row's fallback, never a skill: a skill's design doc and its own commits would put the issue's words in the public diff and history under messages that are not the cover.
